Through this project, I have self-studied the fundamentals of machine learning and deep learning, and can follow the key formulas and model architectures in research papers. So far, I have reviewed several related papers, gained an understanding of the field's development and representative models, and successfully reproduced the nnU-Net baseline on the SPIDER dataset.

Going forward, I plan to explore text prompt generation methods and investigate the application of large models in the medical domain.
